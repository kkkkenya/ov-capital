OVCAPITAL — PHOTO GUIDE
======================
Drop your photos into THIS folder using the exact filenames below.
Until a file exists, the site shows a labeled box describing what belongs there.
Keep each photo under ~300 KB (export JPG at 1600px wide, quality 70-80).
Dark, warm, gold-toned photos match the site best. Avoid screenshots of P&L
or anything that looks like a profit promise.

1. hero-trading-desk.jpg (16:9, wide)
   HERO, top of the homepage under the buttons.
   Best: dark professional trading desk, 2-3 monitors with candlestick
   charts (XAU/USD if possible), warm desk lamp glow. Landscape.

2. about-live-analysis.jpg (3:2)
   ABOUT section, beside "Trading is a professional skill".
   Best: over-the-shoulder shot of a trader studying charts on a big
   monitor, chart glow lighting the room. Shows real analysis work.

3. founder-oluwa-vincent.jpg (4:5, portrait)
   FOUNDER card. Most important photo on the site.
   Best: professional headshot of Oluwa Vincent, dark neutral background,
   smart shirt, confident relaxed expression. Portrait orientation.

4. offerings-mentorship.jpg (16:9, wide)
   WHAT WE OFFER, above the 8 program cards.
   Best: 1-on-1 or small-group coaching moment — mentor and trader
   reviewing a chart on a laptop/screen together.

5. why-discipline.jpg (4:5, portrait)
   WHY OVCAPITAL, beside "consistency beats luck".
   Best: close-up of hands writing in a trading journal, laptop with
   charts and a coffee beside it. Calm, focused, disciplined mood.

6. promise-gold.jpg (16:9, wide)
   OUR PROMISE, under "Learn. Trade. Grow."
   Best: symbolic wealth shot — gold bars/coins macro, or a blurred
   stock ticker board. No text overlays, no specific money amounts.

7. community-meetup.jpg (16:9, wide)
   COMMUNITY, under the Join buttons.
   Best: real group photo from a trader meetup, workshop or class —
   people engaged and learning together. Your strongest trust signal,
   so use a genuine photo, not a stock image, if you have one.

TIP: you only need 5-10. If short on photos, priorities are
3 (founder), 7 (community), 1 (hero), 4 (mentorship), 5 (discipline).
